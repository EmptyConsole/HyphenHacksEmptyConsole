 Idea:
 - Vaccination routes for cities off the grid without healthcare

Problem: Healthcare workers currently cannot find many rural villages that desperatly need vaccinations.
56% of people living in rural areas do not have access to vaccines  - International Labor Organization
3.46 billion people live in rural areas globally - World Bank Group
1.9 billion without vaccines

Related to theme:
We interpreted constellations as connections, and we are now creating connections between rural people and the healthcare they need. The path between stars is long, and by making constellations between them, we are making that path signficnatnly easier to cross.

Why it solves:
Currently health officers don't know where these villages are and how to get to them. We make it so there is a clear path to all the villages that satellites pick up.
Once the officers know where the people are, budgets can finally be reworked to allow for real change.

Details of algorithm:
1. Uses OSM and satellites to locate villages that aren't on official maps
2. Finds points of closest medical center to village
3. Map easiest route of access with cost
4. Calculate cost of country and routes needed



What backend needs to give frontend:
Polygons around map, estimated money needed per polygon, estimated population per polygon
